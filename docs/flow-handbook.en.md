listings that rank and convert — photography, bilingual copy, SEO, publishing,
and ongoing account management — and we can keep running the seller account
afterwards.

**Positioning.** An elite e-commerce content studio helping sellers transform
raw products into high-performing marketplace listings across Egypt and the Arab
world.

**Tagline.** Elevate every listing. Amplify every sale.

**Promise.** From product to marketplace — in one flow.

**What makes us different.** Most sellers have to assemble this themselves: one
photographer, one copywriter, one freelancer who knows Amazon's category rules,
and someone in-house chasing all three. We are a single partner who owns the
whole pipeline, so nothing is dropped in a handoff. We combine AI-assisted
production workflows with human review — the AI moves volume, a person signs off
on what goes live.

**Track record.** Over 210 products photographed and published across eight
categories — accessories, cosmetics, electronics, fashion, home, jewellery,
office, and toys — for brands selling on Egyptian and regional marketplaces.

## 3. The problem we solve

Selling online is harder than it looks. Sellers lose time and revenue because
marketplace management is complex, repetitive, and easy to get wrong.

1. **Technical complexity.** Every platform has different rules for images,
   titles, metadata, and SEO. A listing that passes on Noon can be rejected on
   Amazon.
2. **Time-consuming workflows.** Photography, descriptions, uploads — it adds up
   to hours a seller does not have.
3. **Poor listing performance.** Unoptimized listings miss search traffic and
   fail to convert browsers into buyers. The product is fine; the listing is not.
4. **Scaling challenges.** More products means more chaos. Without a system,
   growth becomes painful — the hundredth product costs more effort than the
   first, not less.

## 5. How we work — the six-step process

1. **Submit your products.** The seller shares product details, existing images,
   or an inventory list. Physical products come to the studio; some categories
   can be shot on location.
2. **Content creation.** We photograph, write, and structure the listings.
   Photography and copy are briefed together so the images and the words tell
   the same story.
3. **Listing optimization.** We optimize titles, keywords, and structure for each
   target platform separately — the same product gets a different title on
   Amazon than on Noon.
4. **Marketplace publishing.** We upload and publish directly to the seller's
   accounts. The seller keeps ownership of their accounts throughout; we work
   inside them.
5. **Account management.** We monitor performance and manage updates on the
   seller's behalf — pricing, stock, returns, compliance, reporting.
6. **Sales growth.** Optimized listings drive more visibility, clicks, and
   conversions. Performance is reviewed on a cadence set by the plan.

Steps 1–4 are the content engagement. Steps 5–6 are the ongoing relationship. A
seller can buy the first half only.

## 8. Commercial models

Three ways to buy, in ascending commitment.

### 8.1 Per-item content (one-time)

Priced per product. The seller pays only for what they send. Three options:
listing copy alone, studio photography alone, or the combined package — which is
cheaper than buying the two separately and is briefed as one job.

No ongoing account support is included. This is a production engagement with a
start and an end.

### 8.2 Account management (monthly)

A fixed monthly fee plus a share of the seller's sales. Two tiers: a baseline
tier that keeps the store running (setup, uploads, returns, reports) and a
deeper tier that also grows it (FBA, purchase orders, Brand Registry, price
analysis).

Content is billed separately per item on this model.

### 8.3 Full growth plans (monthly, all-in)

Everything we do — listing optimization, studio photography, and full account
management — bundled into one monthly plan, for less than buying each piece on
its own. Three tiers by catalogue size, each with an included product count, a
sales share, and a reduced rate for products beyond the included count.

Every growth plan includes the seller's own client dashboard and a performance
report. Higher tiers add a dedicated account manager, a faster review cadence,
and priority production.

### 8.4 How the sales share works

This is the part sellers ask about most, so be precise:

- The share is a percentage of **sales made through the accounts we manage**, on
  top of the fixed monthly fee.
- On the entry growth tier the share is **flat** — it does not move, whatever
  happens.
- On the higher growth tiers the share **starts low and only rises as we hit the
  seller's agreed KPIs**, and it is **capped**. It cannot exceed the cap under
  any circumstances.
- The escalation is earned, not automatic. If we do not hit the KPIs, the share
  stays at its starting level.

The intent is that we get paid more only when the seller is making more. A
seller whose sales do not grow never pays the higher share.

### 8.5 Going over your product count

On a growth plan, products beyond the plan's included count are billed at that
plan's **reduced** extra-item rate — never at the full à-la-carte price. The
bigger the plan, the lower that rate. Going over is not a penalty.

## 9. Policies

- **On-location shoots.** Studio photography is the default and is what the
  standard rate covers. Shooting at the seller's location incurs additional
  transportation fees, quoted per job.
- **Products that cannot travel.** Large, fragile, perishable, or high-value
  items are handled case by case — usually an on-location shoot.
- **Account ownership.** The seller owns their marketplace accounts. We work
  inside them with granted access. We do not hold accounts hostage, and access
  can be revoked at any time.
- **Asset ownership.** Photography and copy we produce for a seller belong to
  that seller on delivery.
- **Revisions.** Handled as part of the engagement within reason. A reshoot
  caused by a change in the product itself, or by a brief that changed after
  production, is scoped separately.
- **Platform rejections.** If a marketplace rejects a listing we produced for a
  compliance reason, we fix and resubmit it. That is part of the job.
- **Currency and billing.** All pricing is in Egyptian Pounds (EGP). Monthly
  plans are billed monthly; per-item work is billed per batch.

## 10. What we do not do

Be straight about the edges. We do not:

- Source products, hold inventory, or handle fulfilment logistics ourselves
  (though we manage FBA inventory *within* the seller's Amazon account)
- Run paid advertising as a standalone service
- Build custom Shopify themes or do general web development
- Guarantee a specific sales figure, search ranking, or "page one" placement —
  no honest partner can
- Guarantee marketplace approval of a product that violates platform policy
- Work on counterfeit, restricted, or non-compliant goods

If a prospect wants something on this list, say so plainly and point them at
what we *can* do.

## 14. Glossary

- **Listing** — a single product's page on a marketplace: title, images, bullets,
  description, and attributes.
- **SEO (marketplace)** — optimizing a listing for the *marketplace's own* search
  engine, which ranks differently from Google.
- **FBA** — Fulfilment by Amazon. Amazon stores and ships the seller's inventory.
- **PO (purchase order)** — a formal order placed with a supplier or with Amazon
  Vendor.
- **Brand Registry** — Amazon's brand-protection programme; unlocks enhanced
  content and counterfeit enforcement.
- **Catalogue** — the seller's full set of products on a platform.
- **Conversion rate** — the share of listing visitors who buy.
- **Sales share** — the percentage of managed sales FLOW earns on top of a fixed
  monthly fee.
- **Extra item** — a product beyond a growth plan's included count, billed at that
  plan's reduced rate.
- **Batch** — one group of products moving through production together.
